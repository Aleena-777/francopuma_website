Website goals and objectives:
Establish an online presence.
Showcase products and services to a wider audience.
Create customer inquiries and leads.
Build credibility and a professional business image.

Key Features and functionality:
Homepage: Overview of the business, highlights, navigation menu, pictures such as the logo or owner.
About us: Business history, leadership, mission statement, vision statement
Products: hardware/building materials catalogue
Enquiry page: Enquiry form for inquires of products and services.
Contact page: Contact information, location form, and contact page.
