Our Story

Founded by Francis Kwabla Nefi, Francopuma Enterprises began operations in 2013 and was formally registered in 2015.It started as a supplier of agrochemicals and agricultural inputs to local farmers. Recognizing the impact of inconsistent weather patterns (including recurring droughts) on agricultural demands, and a growing need for construction materials, the business expanded into hardware and building materials and slowly began phasing out their sales of agrochemicals and agricultural inputs.

The organizations mission and vision are similarly aligned as it is to serve as a trusted supplier of building materials and hardware, enabling builders to construct with confidence.
